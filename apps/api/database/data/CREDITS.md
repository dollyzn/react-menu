Items without a photo show the menu's placeholder until the store uploads a
real one (panel: the item's photo field takes a picture with the phone). The
store's review (2026-11-06) asks for real photos that show the filling or the
exact package; photos that showed another product (empanadas, puff pastry, a
glass instead of the can, another flavor), ingredients the recipe doesn't
have, or the same scene for two flavors were removed. The ones left are
illustrative: a real fried pastel or the right can.
